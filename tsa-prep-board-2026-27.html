Dragster & Hybrid Racer time trials

Dragster DesignHybrid Racer XL

Straight talk: there's no free online contest for either of these, because you can't race a car over the internet. The practice has to happen in the shop — build to the TSA spec, run heats against each other, log every time, and cut weight between runs.

Read this part twice: Dragster is two individuals per chapter, and Hybrid Racer XL is one team of two to four. Both are among the tightest caps on the whole slate. Every logged time trial between now and spring is how those spots get decided, so treat the practice runs as the tryout, because they are.

Worth asking your adviser about: SkillsUSA Maryland runs a separate circuit with overlapping events, and nothing stops you from competing in both.

Two events on the slate are middle school names

The 2027 & 2028 high school eligibility chart doesn't have an event called Digital Photography — that's the middle school one. The high school event is Photographic Technology, and it's one individual per chapter, not a team. Whoever wants it is competing for a single seat.

Coding is the same story: it's on the middle school chart, and the high school equivalent is Software Development (one team per chapter). But TSA's own state-approval page still lists Coding under high school, so those two official pages disagree — your adviser needs to settle it with the state before registration, not after.

You can't enter these alone

Artificial Intelligence is one team of exactly two per chapter. Board Game Design, Fashion Design and Technology and Hybrid Racer XL all need teams of two to four. If you're planning to solo one of these, you need partners before May 12 — after that the roster is frozen.

The tightest seats on the slate

Four events are capped at one or two entries for the entire chapter: Photographic Technology (1 individual), On Demand Video (1 team), Software Development (1 team), Hybrid Racer XL (1 team), and Dragster (2 individuals). Everything else is capped at the state level, which means you're competing against other chapters rather than each other.

Nothing on this board feeds Music Production directly. The NAMT Musical Theater Songwriting Challenge is free and open to high schoolers — different genre, same production chain — but read the rules yourself before committing. Find something better and it goes on the board.

Eligibility and caps are from TSA's High School Competitive Events Guide for the 2027 and 2028 National Conferences. Contest deadlines were confirmed Sept 4, 2026 from each organization's own rules page — but always verify on the official site before you build anything, because dates move.
