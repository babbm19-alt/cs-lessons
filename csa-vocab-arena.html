How the arena works

  • Three kinds of rounds: match a definition to its term, catch the right definition for a term, or name the concept a piece of real code is showing.
  • Scoring: 100 points per correct answer, plus 10 for every second left on the clock.
  • Streaks multiply: 3 in a row ×1.2 · 4 ×1.5 · 5+ ×2. One miss resets it.
  • Run out of time and the clock takes the round — no points lost, streak resets, the answer gets explained.
